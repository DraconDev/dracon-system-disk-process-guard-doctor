# Read-only rehearsal and correction

One fresh-context reviewer inspected the assessment, raw incident/current data,
evidence calculator/fixtures and existing zram source. Its first attempt hit a
provider failure; the retained session was resumed and returned findings. No
reset time was inferred and no alternate CLI/provider was silently substituted.

It corroborated the five separate memory/swap quantities, historical figures,
absence of exclusive paging attribution or promised savings, positional unknown
handling and production-CLI limitations. It found one concrete numeric error:
the draft inferred an exact twice-RAM relationship and used an incorrect
MemTotal byte value. The final assessment instead uses the actual S0 MemTotal
31,914,628 KiB × 1024 = 32,680,579,072 bytes and says approximately twice.
It does not infer the reason for the small difference from device size.

The reviewer had no execution tool and could not rerun calculations/tests or
independently verify committed production-source immutability. These limits
are retained, not presented as independent execution. Parent-run tests,
reproducibility/hash/citation checks and a pre-goal Git source comparison are
recorded in verification.log/json. Subsequent frame fixtures explicitly protect
logical capacity versus allocated RAM and unknown read errors. This rehearsal
is supporting evidence, not final completion approval.
